# Dapur Gadong — WhatsApp Ordering & QR Payment Storefront

**Try the Live App:** https://dapur-gadong.bolt.host/

## Why I Built This
Home-based food vendors, cafes, and Instagram sellers in Brunei waste hours every day answering repetitive DMs, calculating delivery fees, and chasing bank transfer receipts. Most online stores are too complicated for local buyers who just want to order quickly on WhatsApp.

## How It Helps Your Business
* **Easy Mobile Menu:** Customers browse your items, pick add-ons, and build their order right on their phone—no app download needed.
* **Built-In BIBD & Baiduri QR Payment:** Shows your bank QR code at checkout and lets customers upload their transfer receipt immediately.
* **One-Click WhatsApp Order:** Automatically sends a neat, ready-to-read order summary (items, total in BND, delivery address, and payment proof) straight to your WhatsApp.
* **Simple Order & Stock Tracking:** Keeps all your incoming orders and available menu items organized in one place.

## Best For
* Home-based food & dessert sellers
* Cafes, bakeries, and pop-up stalls
* Instagram boutiques and local online shops

## Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS (Progressive Web App / Mobile-First UI)
* **Backend & Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL database for product catalog, order logs, and receipt storage)
* **Integrations:** WhatsApp (`wa.me`) Deep-Linking & Local Bank QR Payment Flow
